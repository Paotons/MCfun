[title]Description[/title]

Export can package the project into a behavior pack directly usable by [code]Minecraft[/code].

[title]Settings[/title]

[b]Include annotations:[/b] Export annotations as well.
[b]Include empty lines:[/b] Export lines with no content.

[b]Note:[/b] Some versions of [code]Minecraft[/code] do not support annotations; confirm before exporting annotations.

[title]Exported files:[/title]

[b]manifest.json :[/b] The main file, including the project name and version.
[b]functions/* :[/b] Functions, including all exported functions.
