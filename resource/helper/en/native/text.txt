[title]Description[/title]
Native commands are not part of Minecraft itself. They are stored inside the software and act as its extensions. They are converted to Minecraft commands on export.

A native command begins with [code]&[/code]. For example, [code]&tp[/code].

[title]Categories[/title]

[file native/tp]Teleport tp[/file]
[file native/scoreboard]Score / Scoreboard scoreboard[/file]
[file native/fill]Fill fill[/file]
[file native/string]String string[/file]
